Using T-SQL in Stack Exchange


SELECT p.Title, p.Id AS [Post Link], u.Id AS [User Link], p.Score, p.ViewCount, u.DisplayName
FROM Posts p
JOIN Users u ON p.OwnerUserId = u.Id
WHERE p.PostTypeId = 1
  AND u.DisplayName = 'BhishanPoudel'
  AND p.Score > 10
