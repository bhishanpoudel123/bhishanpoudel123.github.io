How to Travel New York

A practical plan for a first trip to New York City.

1. Pick your dates and budget

  • Best seasons: late April–June, September–early November.
  • Avoid: Thanksgiving week, Christmas–New Year, peak summer heat.
  • Budget: $150–$300/day per person (hotel + food + transit + one attraction).

2. Book transport

Flying in

  • JFK → AirTrain + subway ($11) or taxi (~$70 flat).
  • LGA → Q70 bus + subway, or taxi (~$40–60).
  • EWR → NJ Transit + AirTrain, or rideshare (~$80).

Train / bus

  • Amtrak to Penn Station (Midtown) — central and easy.
  • Megabus / FlixBus to Port Authority — cheapest option.

3. Where to stay

  • Midtown — walkable to Times Square, Central Park, Broadway.
  • Lower Manhattan — near 9/11 Memorial, Wall St, ferries.
  • Brooklyn (Williamsburg/DUMBO) — cheaper, great views, 10 min by subway.
  • Long Island City — best value, 1 stop from Midtown.

4. Get around

  • Use the subway — 24/7, fastest way.
  • Pay with OMNY: tap any contactless card or phone at the turnstile ($2.90/ride, capped $34/week).
  • Or buy a MetroCard at any station.
  • Download the MTA app or Citymapper for directions.
  • Avoid taxis in rush hour; walk when possible.

5. Must-see (5-day plan)

  1. Day 1 – Midtown: Times Square, Broadway show, Rockefeller Center.
  2. Day 2 – Central Park: Bethesda Terrace, The Met, Fifth Avenue.
  3. Day 3 – Downtown: 9/11 Memorial, Wall St, Statue of Liberty ferry, Brooklyn Bridge walk.
  4. Day 4 – Brooklyn: DUMBO, Brooklyn Bridge Park, Williamsburg food scene.
  5. Day 5 – Culture: MoMA or American Museum of Natural History, High Line, Chelsea Market.

6. Food you should try

  • NY-style pizza slice (Joe's, Prince St, Scarr's).
  • Bagel with lox (Russ & Daughters, Ess-a-Bagel).
  • Pastrami sandwich (Katz's Delicatessen).
  • Chinatown dumplings (Nom Wah Tea Parlor).
  • Halal cart chicken over rice (53rd & 6th).

7. Money-saving tips

  • Buy a CityPASS or NYC Pass if visiting 3+ attractions.
  • Many museums have pay-what-you-wish hours (check the website).
  • Walk the Brooklyn Bridge instead of paying for a tour.
  • Take the Staten Island Ferry (free) for Statue of Liberty views.
  • Eat lunch at food trucks and halal carts — dinner prices are higher.

8. Packing checklist

  • Comfortable walking shoes (you'll do 15,000+ steps/day).
  • Reusable water bottle.
  • Layered clothing — weather changes fast.
  • Small backpack or crossbody bag.
  • Portable charger for your phone.

Safety & etiquette

  • Keep your phone and wallet secure on the subway.
  • Don't block sidewalks — New Yorkers walk fast.
  • Tipping: 18–20% at sit-down restaurants, $1–2 per drink at bars.
  • Emergency number: 911.
