How to Use USPS

A quick guide to shipping, tracking, and using USPS services.

1. Decide what you're sending

  • Letter / card → First-Class Mail (stamp).
  • Small box (under 70 lb) → Priority Mail or Ground Advantage.
  • Urgent → Priority Mail Express (1–2 days).
  • International → Priority Mail International or First-Class Package International.

2. Get a price

  1. Go to usps.com.
  2. Click Quick Tools → Calculate a Price.
  3. Enter origin ZIP, destination ZIP, weight, and dimensions.
  4. Pick the cheapest service that meets your deadline.

3. Ship it — three options

Option A: At the post office counter

  1. Pack the item; seal with tape.
  2. Write the to and from addresses clearly.
  3. Bring it to the counter; pay and get a receipt with tracking.

Option B: Self-service kiosk (APC)

  1. Place the package on the scale.
  2. Tap the screen → choose service → pay by card.
  3. Stick the printed label on the box, drop it in the bin.

Option C: Print label at home

  1. On usps.com, choose Click-N-Ship.
  2. Enter addresses and package details, pay online.
  3. Print the label (regular printer + tape, or label paper).
  4. Schedule a free pickup or drop it in a blue collection box / post office.

4. Track your package

  1. Keep the tracking number from your receipt or label.
  2. Go to usps.com/tracking.
  3. Paste the number → Track.
  4. Or text the number to 28777 for SMS updates.

5. Useful tips

  • Free Priority Mail boxes and envelopes are shipped to your home from usps.com.
  • Always keep the receipt until the package is delivered.
  • Add insurance for items worth more than $100.
  • Use a USPS Change of Address when you move (usps.com/move).
  • Buy Forever stamps — they always cover First-Class letters.

Common issues

  • Package stuck in "Pre-Shipment" → label printed but not yet scanned; wait 24 hrs.
  • "Delivered" but not received → check neighbors, then file a Missing Mail search.
  • Wrong address → file a Package Intercept if not delivered yet.
